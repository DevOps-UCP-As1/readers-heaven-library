Read, share and participate

Events & activities

Meet other readers, discover new ideas and take part in welcoming activities for all ages.

Every first Saturday

Community book club

Share thoughts about the month’s selected book and hear different perspectives from fellow readers.

Time: 11:00 AM–12:30 PM
Venue: Reading Hall
Registration: Sign up at reception.

Second Wednesday

Author talk

Join a discussion with a local writer about storytelling, the writing process and reading culture.

Time: 4:00 PM–5:00 PM
Venue: Community Room
Registration: Reserve a seat at reception.

Every Friday

Children’s story time

A fun session of picture books, read-aloud stories and simple activities for young readers.

Time: 4:00 PM–4:45 PM
Venue: Children’s Corner
Registration: Parent or guardian registers at the desk.

Quarterly

Reading challenge

Set a reading goal, explore different genres and celebrate your progress with the library community.

Time: Details announced each quarter
Venue: Library-wide
Registration: Collect a challenge card at reception.

Seasonal

Literary competition

Showcase your creativity through poetry, short stories or book reviews. Separate age groups may be arranged.

Time: Announced before each event
Venue: Community Room
Registration: Submit your entry at reception.

Monthly

Study & skills workshop

Learn practical skills such as research basics, note-taking, digital literacy and effective study habits.

Time: Last Saturday, 2:00 PM–3:30 PM
Venue: Study Room
Registration: Sign up at reception.
