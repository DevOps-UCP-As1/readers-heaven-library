More than books

Library services

Use our learning and digital facilities to research, study, read online and get everyday tasks done.

Anytime browsing

Online catalogue

Search the library catalogue by title, author or subject. Visit the circulation desk with a title to check availability and borrowing status.

Access: During library opening hours.

Digital reading

E-books

Ask staff for access to the library’s approved digital reading resources. Sign in with your membership details where required.

Access: Library members; resource availability may vary.

Quiet study

Study-room booking

Request a study-room slot at the reception desk. Provide your membership number, preferred date and time, then wait for confirmation.

Booking: Monday–Saturday, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM.

Practical support

Printing & scanning

Bring your document on a USB drive or send it using the instructions provided by staff. Confirm page count and charges before printing.

Hours: 10:00 AM–6:00 PM.

Stay connected

Free Wi-Fi

Ask the reception desk for the current guest Wi-Fi details. Please use the connection responsibly and respect other visitors.

Access: During library opening hours.

Reader assistance

Research assistance

Library staff can help you locate books, use reference materials and get started with reliable research resources.

Support: Ask at the information desk.
